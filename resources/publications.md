# Magazines, journals, and research discovery

Checked 2026-10-05. Recommendations below are editorial judgments based on each publisher's or venue's own description. Start with two regular sources rather than trying to follow everything.

## Magazines: context and accessible explanations

| Resource | Level | Why follow it | Important things to learn | Suggested use / access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [IEEE Spectrum — Robotics](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/robotics) | All levels | Reporting on robotic systems and applications | **Connect the demonstrated behavior to sensors, actuators, and engineering constraints.** Follow linked original research for technical claims. | One article weekly; web access varies by article. |
| [IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ram/) | Beginner with prerequisites → advanced | Accessible technical robotics articles and system perspectives | **System architecture, integration tradeoffs, experimental setup, and deployment lessons.** | One tutorial or system article monthly; institutional access may help. |
| [AAAI AI Magazine](https://aaai.org/ai-magazine/) | Beginner with AI vocabulary → advanced | Expository articles across AI, useful beyond your immediate specialty | **Problem formulation, reasoning, evaluation, and how subfields connect.** | One survey monthly; [AAAI's author guidance](https://aaai.org/ai-magazine/ai-magazine-information-for-authors/) describes open access for articles published after 2022-01-01. |

**My recommended starting pair:** Spectrum for a quick view of the field, plus Robotics & Automation Magazine for more technical explanations. Add AI Magazine when you want a broader AI perspective.

## Journals: technical depth

| Resource | Level | What it offers | What to learn from the papers |
|---|---|---|---|
| [IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ieee-robotics-and-automation-letters/) | Intermediate → advanced | Concise reports of robotics research | **Identify the concrete contribution, its baseline, and the evaluation that supports it.** |
| [IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), through the RAS publication directory](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/) | Advanced | In-depth robotics treatments | **Derivations, assumptions, ablations, and how a complete system is justified.** |
| [Science Robotics](https://www.science.org/journal/scirobotics) | Intermediate overview → advanced | Broad scientific robotics research | **Physical mechanisms, experimental evidence, and connections between hardware and intelligence.** Publisher page could not be read automatically in this session; [publisher editorial describing its scope](https://doi.org/10.1126/SCIROBOTICS.AAS9540). |
| [Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR)](https://www.jmlr.org/) | Intermediate → advanced | Openly accessible ML research | **Statistical assumptions, optimization, generalization, and reproducible algorithms.** Select by topic rather than reading every issue. |

IEEE and Science article access can vary; try your university library and author-hosted versions. No paid subscription is necessary to begin with the free courses and papers in this library.

## Find original papers

- [Robotics: Science and Systems proceedings](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/): browse papers and select one whose method you can connect to a small project.
- [Proceedings of Machine Learning Research](https://proceedings.mlr.press/): hosts conference and workshop volumes, including robot-learning and general ML work. Check the specific volume and paper.
- [IEEE RAS publications directory](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/): navigate the official robotics publication portfolio.

**Reading habit:** extract the question, method, evidence, and failure cases. Keep news, author-reported results, and your own conclusions distinguishable in your notes.
