# Source and verification notes

**Edition date: 2026-10-05, America/Chicago.**

## Selection method

The library follows the user's preference for balanced robotics and AI resources at all useful levels. Primary sources were used: author-hosted project pages, arXiv abstracts and HTML papers, university course pages, official documentation, and publisher or venue pages.

Paper notes are selective summaries and study guidance. Full-text versions are linked when inspected. An inspected version may be older than the current arXiv revision; the note identifies that version through its link. The notes are not exhaustive literature reviews or independent replications.

## Recent versus foundational

- **2026:** Xiaomi-Robotics-0 and the VLA datasets/benchmarks survey.
- **2025:** DreamerV3's Nature article, π0.5, SmolVLA, and Depth Anything 3.
- **2023–2024:** ACT, Diffusion Policy, DROID, OpenVLA, and TD-MPC2.
- **Foundations:** RRT*, information-theoretic MPC, LIO-SAM, and ORB-SLAM3.

Release year is distinct from acceptance year. For example, ORB-SLAM3 appeared as a 2020 preprint and has a 2021 T-RO DOI; DreamerV3 is represented here by its 2025 Nature article, not presented as an algorithm invented in 2025.

## Publication status

Verified venue information is given for ACT and Diffusion Policy using the authors' pages, LIO-SAM using the arXiv comment, ORB-SLAM3 using its related publisher DOI, and DreamerV3 using Nature.

For other entries, this edition records the arXiv paper/report and does not infer acceptance from the arXiv listing. “Peer-review status not verified” does not mean rejected or necessarily unpublished.

## Access limitations found during checking

- Science Robotics' landing page could not be fetched automatically; its scope is supported by a publisher editorial.
- ROS documentation presented an automated-access challenge; the official release announcement supports the tutorial entry point.
- The notebook robotics book was verified through search results and its authors' repository when its home page did not yield readable content.
- Nav2's entry page yielded a redirect without readable documentation in the browsing tool.
- TD-MPC2's project site did not yield readable content, so the note relies on the paper's HTML and abstract. No unverified code-repository URL is included.

These are limits of this session's automatic verification, not conclusions that the resources are unavailable to you.

## What is original guidance

Difficulty levels, priorities, exercises, the study plan, and paragraphs labeled “My assessment” are editorial recommendations. Numerical benchmark claims have generally been avoided so the library emphasizes concepts and evaluation design.

Original full texts, videos, code, datasets, and model weights stay at their linked sources. Check the relevant source's license before redistributing material.
