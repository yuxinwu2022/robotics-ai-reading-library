## Summary

This survey organizes VLA research around datasets, benchmarks, and data-generation engines. It distinguishes real and synthetic data and analyzes embodiment, modalities, action conventions, and evaluation structure. It argues for stronger physical grounding and better tests of reasoning and long-horizon generalization.

## Important parts to learn

- **MUST LEARN: align data collection with the capability being tested.**
- **MUST LEARN: environment splits, task composition, and action-space conventions.**
- **ADVANCED: physical grounding and scalable data generation.**
