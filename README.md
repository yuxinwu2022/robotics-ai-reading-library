## How the collection is marked

- **MUST LEARN** identifies a reusable concept worth understanding and explaining.
- **ADVANCED** marks material for a second pass.
- Levels describe prerequisites, not academic degree requirements.
- Publication status is recorded separately from usefulness. ArXiv availability alone does not establish peer review.
- Every paper note includes a summary, learning priorities, reading focus, prerequisites, a suggested exercise, and a critical-reading prompt.

## Recent research and foundations

The selection includes **2025–2026** papers on world models, efficient VLAs, learned geometry, asynchronous execution, and data infrastructure. Older papers are labeled as foundations and selected for enduring educational value. This is a curated starter collection, not an exhaustive list or a claim to include the latest paper in every subfield.

Coverage includes planning, control, SLAM, perception, manipulation, imitation learning, model-based reinforcement learning, and research evaluation. Legged robotics, tactile sensing, soft robotics, and human–robot interaction are useful directions for later expansion.

## Sources and scope

[Source and verification notes](resources/source-notes.md) explain how references were checked. The repository stores resource links and original study summaries; full papers, books, model weights, and datasets remain with their original publishers or authors.

Exercises are proposals for your study, not experiments already run. No recurring updates have been scheduled.
